# ggyoudraw (development version)

* `you_draw_it()` gains `auto_reveal`: show the real line as soon as the reader
  has drawn the whole line, without a button.
* `you_draw_it()` gains `controls`, to put the buttons above the chart.
* The reveal button pulses briefly once the line is complete.
* `geom_you_draw_line()` gains `zone_fill`, `known_fill` and `fill_alpha`, to
  choose the fills of the drawing zone and of the part with the known line.
